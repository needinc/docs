---
title: "Cancer Protection System"
---

Need Cancer Protection System protects policyholder through three modes:

- **Healthy Mode**: Cancer risk, screening, early detection, prevention, and ongoing engagement before a diagnosis.
- **Treatment** **Mode**: Support across diagnosis, treatment decisions, specialist selection, second opinions, clinical trials, care navigation, and treatment monitoring.
- **Recovery Mode**: Surveillance, recurrence monitoring, long-term treatment effects, rehabilitation, and health after treatment.

![CPS Architecture](/images/CPS-architecture.jpg "CPS Architecture")

**Definitions**

- **Policyholder Product**: The product for policyholders, designed to support them throughout their cancer journey to achieve the best cancer outcomes
- **Hero Product**: The product for Heroes to empower them to deliver the best cancer outcomes for their patients
- **Agentic System**: The system of AI agents that drives the product with supporting systems
- **Data Infrastructure:** The system that stores, organizes, and secures data across the organization
